Project & Location

OsdagBridge is developed under the FOSSEE project at IIT Bombay.

FOSSEE Project

Free and Open Source Software for Education (FOSSEE) is developed at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, funded by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Visit fossee.in →

Osdag Initiative

Osdag promotes open-source cross-platform structural steel design tools, empowering civil and structural engineers, educators, and researchers worldwide.

Visit osdag.fossee.in →
OsdagBridge

Open-source steel girder bridge analysis and design, part of the Osdag ecosystem for structural engineering education, research, and practice.
